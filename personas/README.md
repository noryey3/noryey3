Works for **any service** — accounting, legal, social media, recruiting, IT, marketing, logistics, and beyond.

## The Core Idea

People don't buy services. They buy solutions to situations they're already in.

There are five recurring buyer situations that show up across every industry, every service, every B2B market. Learn to recognize which situation a prospect is in — and you instantly know what they care about, what they're afraid of, and how to frame your offer.

**This system tells you:**
1. Is this lead actually worth reaching out to? → `winnability-framework.md`
2. Which situation are they in? → `profiles/`
3. How do I position my offer so they feel stupid saying no? → `positioning-guide.md`
4. How do I adapt this for a new client or service? → `how-to-customize.md`

## How to Research a Lead Before You Send (the 5-min version)

1. **Bio / website (30 sec)** — What do they sell? Do they have a real business?
2. **Last 12 posts (60 sec)** — Any pain signals? How active are they?
3. **Podcast / content output (30 sec)** — Is anything obviously broken or inconsistent?
4. **LinkedIn (30 sec)** — What's their role? Can they make a buying decision?
5. **Score them (60 sec)** — Use the winnability framework
6. **Find one specific thing (60 sec)** — One detail to put in the opening line

**If you can't find a signal in 5 minutes — skip and move on.**

## Hormozi Principles Built Into This System

- **Don't convince. Find.** You're not trying to create desire. You're finding people who already have the problem.
- **Score before you send.** 50 messages to 8/10 leads beats 500 messages to 4/10 leads.
- **Name their situation.** The best opening line isn't about your service — it's a reflection of their exact current reality.
- **Remove all risk.** The offer isn't complete until there's a reason to try it that costs them nothing.
- **Follow up exactly twice.** More than that and you're a nuisance. Less than that and you're leaving money on the table.
