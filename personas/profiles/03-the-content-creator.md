# P03 — The Content Creator

> "I'm already making content everywhere. I just need the podcast to run itself."

---

## Who They Are

A YouTuber, TikToker, Instagrammer, or blogger who is adding (or thinking about adding) a podcast as another content channel. They understand content deeply — they move fast, think in output, and are obsessed with repurposing. Budget is tight because podcast monetization hasn't kicked in yet, but they're entrepreneurial enough to see the ROI when you show it to them.

**Typical roles:** Content Creator, YouTuber, Influencer, Online Educator, Blogger, Brand Strategist
**Industries:** Lifestyle, Gaming, Finance, Fitness, Travel, Personal Development, Entertainment
**Budget:** Under $500–$1,000/mo

---

## Dream Outcome

They want their existing content to automatically become a podcast — zero extra work, zero extra recordings, zero extra editing. One video shoot becomes a YouTube video, a podcast episode, and an audiogram for Instagram. Their audience grows on a platform they're currently ignoring. And they didn't have to touch anything new.

---

## Biggest Nightmare

Adding a podcast is just adding more to an already maxed-out schedule. They record an episode, it sits there unedited for 3 weeks, they finally upload it but the audio is rough, the show notes don't exist, and after two months they stop publishing. Now they have a dead podcast collecting dust that makes them look inconsistent.

This has happened to them before. They want it to actually work this time.

---

## Winnability by Default: 6/10

Good prospect but price-sensitive. They need ROI to be made obvious. The positioning must lead with repurposing (they already have the content), not with quality (they're not audiophiles). They're reachable and responsive on social media — easier to get in contact with than most personas.

---

## Green Lights (Raise Score to 8–10)

- They have a YouTube channel or Instagram with existing video/audio content that could become podcast episodes
- Their audience has mentioned wanting a podcast ("You should have a podcast!")
- They post frequently — their content output is already high but podcast is missing
- They mention "repurposing" content or being interested in omnichannel
- They have a product/course — the podcast is a low-cost acquisition channel for them

## Red Flags (Lower Score or Skip)

- Strict hobbyist — no business model and no interest in one
- Budget is genuinely $0 — they're in pure growth phase with no income
- They don't have existing content to repurpose — starting from scratch with a tight budget is harder to justify

---

## Cold Outreach Script

**Channel:** Instagram DM, Twitter/X DM, or YouTube comment — go where they live
**Tone:** Casual, peer-like, energetic — NOT corporate. Sound like a creator talking to a creator.

---

**Subject / Opening DM:**

Hey [Name] — I've been watching your [YouTube / following your Instagram] for a while. The [specific video/post] actually changed how I think about [topic]. Good stuff.

Quick thought: your audience keeps asking you for a podcast (I've seen the comments). Makes sense — your content translates perfectly to audio.

I help creators turn their existing videos/recordings into a published podcast automatically — editing, show notes, Spotify/Apple distribution, the whole thing. You'd just need to send us the recording.

Would that be useful for you? Happy to share what it looks like.

---

**Follow-up #1 (Day 3, DM):**

> Hey — circling back real quick. Even if timing isn't right, happy to show you how some creators are getting their podcast running on autopilot from their existing content. Might be useful context!

**Follow-up #2 (Day 7, final):**

> Last one! If you ever want your existing content turned into a podcast without adding to your workload, I'm here. [Link to calendar or examples]

---

## Discovery Call Questions

1. What platforms are you creating content on right now, and what's performing best?
2. How much existing content (video, audio, written) do you have that could be repurposed into podcast episodes?
3. What's stopped you from having a consistent podcast up until now?
4. How hands-off do you want to be — would you be okay sending a raw recording and having us handle everything from there?
5. What would a successful podcast mean for your brand in the next year — more audience, brand deals, a course funnel?

---

## The Hormozi Reframe for This Persona

They will say: "It's too expensive for me right now."
You say:

> "Makes total sense — let's do the math together. You have [X] thousand followers. If 5% of them follow you to your podcast, that's [X] listeners on day one. If the podcast brings in one brand deal or one course sale this month, what's that worth? Most creators I work with break even on the production fee in the first 30 days once their audience migrates over. The cost of not having a podcast right now is the audience you're not building on audio platforms while your competitors do."
