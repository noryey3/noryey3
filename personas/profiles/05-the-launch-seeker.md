# P05 — The Launch Seeker

> "I've been meaning to start a podcast for 6 months. I just need someone to tell me what to do."

---

## Who They Are

Someone who has DECIDED they want a podcast — the idea is ready, the motivation is real — but the technical overwhelm and analysis paralysis have kept them from starting. They've researched microphones, compared hosting platforms, watched YouTube tutorials, and still haven't hit record. They don't need more information. They need a guide who removes all the decisions and says "here's the plan, follow me."

**Typical roles:** Entrepreneur, Author, Speaker, Coach, Executive, Professional with niche expertise
**Industries:** Coaching, Speaking, Healthcare, Legal, Faith & Community, Fitness, Nonprofit
**Budget:** $1,000–$2,500/mo (launch fee + ongoing retainer)

---

## Dream Outcome

Launch day. Their show is live on Spotify and Apple Podcasts. It sounds incredible — professional music, clean audio, a killer intro. Their email list and social following show up and subscribe. The first 3 episodes are already queued up, edited, and scheduled. They hit publish on episode one feeling proud, not embarrassed.

And 6 months later, clients bring it up in sales calls. "I listened to your podcast before we got on this call." The show is doing its job.

---

## Biggest Nightmare

They finally launch after months of overthinking — and the first episode sounds amateur. The audio is muddy, there's no real structure, the artwork looks generic. Their audience gives it a polite "great job!" and never listens again. They spent all that time and anxiety preparing for a launch that flopped.

Or they never launch at all. Six months from now they're still "thinking about it" while a competitor fills the niche they had a clear claim on.

---

## Winnability by Default: 9/10

This is one of the HIGHEST winnability personas in cold outreach. Why? Because:
- The pain is the DELAY itself — every day they don't launch, they feel it
- The decision is 100% theirs — no committee, no approval
- They have money (they're professionals or entrepreneurs)
- The emotion is already there — you just need to give them a path
- They've essentially pre-sold themselves. You just need to be the person who shows up with a clear plan.

---

## Green Lights (Raise Score to 10/10)

- They've posted "I want to start a podcast" or "thinking about starting a podcast" — buying intent is explicit
- They're launching a book, course, or new business in the next 60–90 days (podcast = launch accelerator)
- They follow podcast-related accounts or engage with podcasting content
- They've asked for microphone recommendations publicly — they're in research mode
- Their niche has NO dominant podcast yet — there's a clear opportunity they might see

## Red Flags (Lower Score or Skip)

- They've tried and abandoned a podcast before with no explanation of why (might repeat the cycle)
- They seem to want to DIY everything — "I just need to know what equipment to buy" — they're not buying yet
- Budget is genuinely unavailable — they're bootstrapping and prioritizing survival

---

## Cold Outreach Script

**Channel:** Email, LinkedIn, Instagram DM — wherever you can find them
**Tone:** Warm, confident, clear. You're the guide. They're looking for someone to take the wheel.

---

**Subject:** Your podcast could be live in 30 days

**Email:**

Hey [Name],

I saw your [post / LinkedIn update / tweet] about wanting to start a podcast — and your audience is clearly ready for it (the comments said so).

Here's what I do: I launch podcasts for [coaches/speakers/professionals like you] in 30 days or less. That means the concept, name, artwork, editing, hosting setup, and distribution to Spotify and Apple — all done. You show up for the recordings. We handle everything else.

Most people sit on the podcast idea for 6–12 months because the setup feels overwhelming. We've done this enough times that there's nothing to figure out — you just follow the roadmap.

If that sounds like what's been missing, a 20-minute call is worth it. Here's my calendar: [link]

[Your name]

---

**Follow-up #1 (Day 3):**

> Hey [Name] — just following up! If the timing isn't right, no worries. But if the podcast idea is still in your head taking up space, that's usually a sign it's ready. Happy to show you what a 30-day launch looks like — no commitment.

**Follow-up #2 (Day 7):**

> Last message! One thought before I go quiet: every month that passes is a month a competitor could claim your niche on audio. If you do want to move on the podcast this year, I'm here. [Calendar link]

---

## Discovery Call Questions

1. Tell me about the podcast concept — what's the show about and who is it for?
2. How long have you been thinking about this? What's kept you from launching so far?
3. Is there a specific date you'd like to launch around — a book, a course, an event?
4. What does your existing audience look like — email list, social, current clients you could launch to?
5. What does success look like 6 months after launch — what would make this feel completely worth it?
6. When you imagine your launch episode going live, what does that look and sound like to you?

---

## The Hormozi Reframe for This Persona

They will say: "I'm not sure I'm ready — I still need to figure out my show concept."
You say:

> "Figuring out the concept is literally step one of the launch package — that's the first thing we do together. You don't need to have it figured out before we start. That's the whole point: you don't need to be ready, you just need to decide. We have a process that walks you through it in week one. The show concept comes out of that process. Most clients say that first session alone is worth more than what they paid — because they finally have clarity on what their show is actually about."
