Real estate, at its best, is quiet work. It is the unhurried walkthrough at dusk. The third draft of an offer letter. The phone call that lasts an hour because the right answer was never simple.

Based in Northern Virginia and licensed across the DC metro, I represent a small, intentional roster of clients each year — buyers seeking a forever home in McLean, sellers preparing a Great Falls estate, families relocating into Tysons. The work is bespoke. The standard is uncompromising.
