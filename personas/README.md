# Persona Generator — Cold Outreach Second Brain

> "The single greatest skill in business is the ability to get strangers to want to give you money." — Alex Hormozi

This system helps you answer three questions before you ever send a cold message:

1. **Is this lead actually winnable?** (Winnability Score)
2. **What does this person REALLY want?** (Dream Outcome)
3. **How do I position my service so they feel stupid saying no?** (Grand Slam Offer angle)

---

## How to Use This

When you're looking at a cold outreach prospect, do this:

1. **Score them** using `winnability-framework.md`
2. **Match them to a persona** in the `profiles/` folder
3. **Read their Dream Outcome and Biggest Nightmare** — internalize it
4. **Use the Cold Outreach Script** in their profile as your base message
5. **Check `positioning-guide.md`** for how to frame your specific service

**Green Light (8–10):** Personalize heavily and reach out today.
**Yellow (5–7):** Worth reaching out — slightly more generic message is okay.
**Red (0–4):** Not ready. Skip or put in a long-term nurture pile.
