# Winnability Framework — Cold Outreach Lead Scoring

> "The dream customer has the problem, can afford the solution, and is actively looking for it." — Alex Hormozi, $100M Leads

Score your lead in under 2 minutes. Add up the points. Make the call.

---

## The Five Criteria

### 1. Problem Awareness — /3 pts

Do they KNOW they have a podcast problem and are they actively feeling it?

| Signal | Points |
|--------|--------|
| They've publicly complained about podcasting (caption, tweet, comment, post) | 3 |
| They have a podcast with obvious quality issues (bad audio, inconsistent, no show notes) | 2 |
| They have a podcast but it looks neglected (last episode 2+ months ago) | 2 |
| They've mentioned wanting to start a podcast publicly | 2 |
| You're guessing they probably have the problem (no direct signal) | 1 |
| No clear signal at all | 0 |

---

### 2. Budget Signal — /3 pts

Can they actually pay? Hormozi's rule: don't try to sell to someone who can't buy.

| Signal | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Runs ads, has a team, sells products/services online — clearly invested in their brand | 3 |
| Has a professional website, active social presence, and obvious business model | 2 |
| Has a following or audience but monetization is unclear | 2 |
| Individual creator — small but growing | 1 |
| Hobbyist with no apparent business model | 0 |

---

### 3. Decision Power — /2 pts

Can they say YES without asking someone else?

| Signal | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Solo founder, personal brand, or individual content creator | 2 |
| Small business owner or founder (company is their brand) | 2 |
| Marketing manager or content lead at a company | 1 |
| Employee with no buying authority | 0 |

---

### 4. Timing — /2 pts

Are they looking NOW, or are you waking them up from a dead sleep?

| Signal | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Recently posted about podcasting, audio quality, or needing help with content | 2 |
| Actively growing their content output — new episodes recently, posting about it | 2 |
| Has a new product/book/course launching (podcast = distribution strategy right now) | 2 |
| Their podcast is stagnant but they still post on social | 1 |
| No recent activity — podcast is dormant | 0 |

---

## Scoring

| Total Score | Rating | What to Do |
|-------------|--------|------------|
| **8–10** | Green Light | Reach out TODAY. Personalized, specific message. This is a hot lead. |
| **5–7** | Yellow | Worth reaching out. Reference one specific thing about them. |
| **3–4** | Warm-Up Needed | Follow them first. Engage with their content for 5–7 days before reaching out. |
| **0–2** | Not Ready | Skip. Don't waste a message on someone who can't buy or doesn't feel the pain. |

## The Hormozi Principle Behind This

The goal of cold outreach is not to convince people to want something they don't want.

**It's to find people who already want it and make them an offer they can't refuse.**

A 10/10 lead is someone already bleeding. You're just offering them a bandage.
A 3/10 lead is someone who might get a paper cut someday. Don't waste your time.

**Volume at quality beats volume at scale.** 50 personalized messages to 8/10 leads will out-convert 500 generic messages to 4/10 leads every time.
